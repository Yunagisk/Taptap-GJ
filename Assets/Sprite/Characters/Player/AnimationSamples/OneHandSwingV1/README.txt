ONE-HAND SWING / TORCH

Open Assets/Scenes/CombatAnimationPreview.unity and press Play.
Torch is selected initially. Click Attack, then the enemy. Switch to the sword
with the existing loadout button to compare the accepted thrust animation.
The preview uses an isolated 10 Fire damage torch and extra player HP.
The production KBW09 torch remains at its existing placeholder damage of 0.
The preview scene is not added to the build scene list.

Motion: 0.90 s, one damage event at 0.36 s, contact hold 0.05 s.
Lift -> accelerating downward sweep -> contact -> follow-through -> recovery.
Feet and character root stay fixed. Idle breathing blends continuously in/out.
The original 2048 x 2048 torch PNG is unchanged. Character and weapon are
separate; the existing body, face, arm IK, and palm drawing are reused.
Only the torch flame deforms. The shaft and grip remain rigid.
Fire trail and impact embers are generated by the existing effect mesh system.
This supplies the torch variant; a flail chain animation is not included.

Power revision: shoulder/elbow rotations now carry the torch swing. Wrist
articulation varies only from 45 to 55 degrees in the source rig (10 degree
range), instead of the previous roughly 80 degree range. Both torch and sword
have deeper anticipation, stronger hip drive and forward lean, counter-rotated
heads and delayed cape follow-through. Sword forearm stretching is corrected.
Timing and damage events are unchanged. Both revised motions were reviewed
in Unity at anticipation/contact and recovered to idle without runtime errors.

Initial validation (Unity 2022.3.62f3c1, Editor Play, temporary 60 FPS cap):
55 attack frames over 0.918 s, 59.91 FPS average, slowest frame 17.50 ms.
Exactly one hit at pose time 0.36 s. Idle restored, effects cleared, no errors.
The measurement cap and temporary debug probe are not shipped in Assets.
Additional checks: project compilation, 80 playback/action regressions, scene
binding preservation, continuous grip registration, and fixed feet/root.

Local review artifacts: output/animation/one-hand-swing/
preview.html is an offline pose preview; it does not render the live flame,
idle breathing, or attack effects. Open the Unity scene for the final result.
